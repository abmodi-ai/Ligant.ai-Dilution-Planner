# B9 — Proposed open items from the build

Places where the URS v0.4.1 leaves a choice the build had to make, where the build and the URS could disagree, or where a requirement could not be met as written. Each is a proposal with reasoning; none is a specification edit.

**Status, 15 September 2026:** every item below was decided under owner delegation — see `docs/decisions.md` (P1 → D-3; P2 → D-7; P3 → D-8; P4 → D-9; P5/P6 → D-10; P7 → D-11; P8–P10 → D-12; P13 → D-2; P16 → D-5; P17 → D-4). The reasoning is kept here for NADIRA's review.

| P1 | Shared result object not in the package | D1, C3-OUT-05, open item 8 | Emits a validated `1-c3-draft` object; acceptance 4 not testable yet | See `docs/D1-shared-object.md`. Ask: the shared-format definition or a C1 object |
| P2 | Independent mode: capacity (iii) on a **shared** intermediate | C3-DT-06 steps 2–4 | Each point takes its smallest g passing (i) and (iii) *for itself*; points are grouped by g; if the shared vessel exceeds C, every member of that group advances to its next candidate together and grouping repeats; a point with no candidate left is rejected under C3-HI-09 naming capacity | The rule is stated per step; sharing is a consequence of step 4. Evaluating (iii) on the shared vessel makes g depend on the other points, and the URS does not say which point moves. Moving the whole group is deterministic and symmetric. Alternative: flag the shared vessel under C3-FL-03 instead. Owner to choose |
| P3 | Target = stock under the **diluent-volume** basis | §7 "a target equal to the stock is legal"; C3-VB-01 | The undiluted point is prepared as the stated volume of stock alone (T = D as a volume of stock, no diluent), C3-FL-09 raised | T = D/(f − 1) is undefined at f = 1. No engine change was needed or made.
