The per-point bound stays on every point and in the object, with status `open`: C3-OUT-03 and C3-IV-07 require a bound at the point, and it is computed from that point's own displayed values, not read from a register. `closureResidual` stays `derived` — it is C3-DT-04 arithmetic, not one of items 6 and 7 |

Deferring the clear to the next frame would delay a spec-mandated clear to fit a test harness |
